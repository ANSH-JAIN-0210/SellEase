# Factory workflows — source observations

These observations describe the 14 supplied reference photographs. They are requirements evidence, not verified accounting records. No handwritten amount has been imported.

## Costing and daybook (images 1–4 and 7)

The photographed daybook is used for style-linked cost breakdowns: fabric, cutting, stitching, thread, elastic, washing, pressing, packaging, helpers, rent/electricity, repairs and overhead appear across pages. Some entries are per-unit costs rather than cash payments. Store costing lines separately from actual expense records and payment postings. Keep category, unit basis, source attachment, style/job reference and approval explicit. Never assume a printed Receipts/Payments column establishes the economic direction of every handwritten line.

## Fabric cutting and size matrix (images 8–9)

Pages associate a style/reference, date, color, fabric lengths and L/XL/XXL quantities with a garment sketch or fabric swatch. Digitize planned and actual color/size quantities and fabric-lot issues. Keep input units explicit. Consumption formulas and abbreviated average calculations require confirmation; reject incompatible units and do not hardcode handwritten formulas.

## Receipts, payments and running balances (images 5, 10–12)

Party registers reference dated bills, cash payments, bank/payment references and running balances. A digital party statement must derive its debit, credit and closing balance from posted journals and source documents. Payment allocation must be independent of invoice creation; corrections require a traceable reversal. Image 11 also shows a separate list of named payees and amounts whose precise business classification is unconfirmed.

## Goods bill and GST invoice (images 6 and 13)

Image 6 has supplier identity, GSTIN, invoice number/date, billed-to details, supply and transport fields, line descriptions, HSN/SAC, quantity, rate, amounts, freight/packing, taxable value, CGST/SGST/IGST, discount and final total. Image 13 is a simpler bill form with supplier/receiver details, quantity, description, HSN, rate and amount. Model document type and tax treatment explicitly; never infer tax applicability from an empty printed template. Bank details in a photograph must not become application defaults.

## Delivery challan (image 14)

The template includes sender/consignee, challan number/date, order number/date, reverse-charge field, quantity, description, HSN, rate/amount, total assessable value, vehicle, e-way bill, gate pass and receiver/checker acknowledgement. Separate dispatch confirmation from drafting and printing. Partial dispatch must reduce stock once and maintain quantities against its sales order.

## End-to-end target

Purchase order → partial goods receipt → fabric/raw-material lot → material issue → cutting size matrix → configurable production stages and quality acceptance → finished stock receipt → sales order → partial dispatch/challan → finalized invoice → payment allocation → reconciled party/cash/bank ledger and reports.

Each stock or accounting posting needs organization, actor, source document, timestamp and a retry-safe identity. Draft documents do not post stock or money.
